Why this pairing matters.

A good demo is not enough. The technology has to work, but it also has to fit the way legal work is actually done.

That is what we are testing. Build it, show it to people in law, listen to what fails, then improve what proves useful.

The research questionnaire and founding pilot are the next part of that process.
