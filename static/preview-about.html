Why build Lylo this way?

Technical capability alone is not enough. A system can look impressive and still fail to fit the way solicitors actually work.

Legal experience alone is not enough either. The product still has to be built, tested and pushed until the useful parts are real.

That is why the next stage is built around research and a small founding pilot: show the system, listen to people in law, test real workflows safely and improve what proves useful.
