About Lylo

The people behind the project.

Lylo combines engineering and legal experience to test one practical question: where can AI genuinely make legal work easier?

Why we are building it.

We are testing where AI can save time inside law firms, where it still needs human judgement and what a useful private legal AI system should actually look like. Our research and founding pilot are how we learn that from people who work in law.

Engineering meets legal practice.

Different backgrounds let us test both whether the technology works and whether it fits the way legal work is actually done.
