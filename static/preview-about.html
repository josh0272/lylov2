About Lylo

The people behind the project.

Lylo is being built as a practical test: can AI take real friction out of legal work without taking control away from the solicitor?

Why we are building it.

We are testing where AI can genuinely save time inside law firms, where it still needs human judgement and what a useful private legal AI system should actually look like. The founding pilot and our research are designed to answer those questions with people who work in law.

Two backgrounds. One test.

Lylo combines a technical build with a legal-user perspective.
