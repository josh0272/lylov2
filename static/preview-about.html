How we are approaching Lylo.

Build it, learn from the people who use legal workflows, then test whether it is actually useful.

1

Build

Turn broad AI ideas into specific demos and legal workflows that people can see and try.

2

Research

Ask solicitors, trainees and law students where time is lost, what they would trust AI to do, and what needs human control.

3

Pilot

Test Lylo on a safe example and compare it with the firm’s normal process before discussing anything paid.
