About Lylo

The people behind the project.

Lylo brings together an engineering build and hands-on legal experience to test where AI can genuinely help law firms.

Why we are building it.

We are testing where AI can save time inside law firms, where it still needs human judgement and what a useful private legal AI system should actually look like. The founding pilot and our research are designed to answer those questions with people who work in law.

Engineering meets legal practice.

Two different backgrounds give us a better way to test both the technology and whether it actually fits legal work.
