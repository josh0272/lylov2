How we are approaching Lylo.

Build something people can see. The demos turn broad AI ideas into specific legal workflows that can be discussed and tested.

Ask the people doing the work. Our questionnaire looks at ET1s, Schedules of Loss, workflow problems, AI concerns, accuracy, speed and the controls people would need before trusting the system.

Test before selling. The founding pilot is designed to compare Lylo with a firm’s normal process on a safe example before discussing a paid pilot.
