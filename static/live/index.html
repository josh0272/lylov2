The problem

AI is changing how people work. Legal work has more to protect.

For most work, using AI can be as simple as dropping in a document and asking a question. A solicitor cannot treat a client file like ordinary text. It may contain confidential, sensitive or privileged information — and responsibility for the work still sits with the solicitor.

01
The useful part is obvious

AI can search documents, pull out facts, compare information and help build a first draft.

02
But the information is different

The documents where AI could be most useful are often the same documents a firm has to protect most carefully.

03
So much of the work stays manual

Solicitors still spend time finding information across the file, moving it into the next document and checking it back against the source.

That is the gap Lylo is being designed for. Useful AI for legal work, designed around keeping client information under the firm’s control — with the solicitor still reviewing, editing and making the decisions.
