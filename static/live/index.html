Firm-controlled by design

Private AI, designed to run inside your firm.

Our aim is to make secure, firm-controlled AI practical for law firms of every size — without asking them to send confidential legal work to a public AI service.

Private deploymentClient information stays within the agreed firm-controlled environment.
No training on your dataLylo is being designed so firm material is not used to train a public AI service.
Source-linked outputsAnswers and drafts keep the supporting document available for review.

The precise safeguards depend on each firm’s deployment, access controls, policies and use. Lylo is being designed around privacy by design, data minimisation and controlled access.

Professional control

AI supports. You decide.

Lylo’s answers, forms and calculations remain drafts for solicitor review, editing and approval. The source stays available so professional judgement remains with the firm.
