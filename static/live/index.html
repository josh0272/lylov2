AI use in legal work is changing.

Law Society of Scotland · Guide to Generative AIEngland & Wales · 17 Aug 2026 · SRA Misuse of AI warning
Legal regulators and professional bodies are increasingly addressing inaccurate AI output, confidentiality, supervision and professional responsibility. Lylo is being designed around those risks.
Accuracy & qualityGenerative AI can produce inaccurate, incomplete or convincing-looking output.Lylo is being designed to ground answers in the firm’s own documents and show the source material for review.
Confidentiality & securityClient information and firm systems need careful protection when AI tools are used.Lylo’s private legal AI is being designed so sensitive case work can remain inside the firm’s environment.
Human oversightAI output still needs proper human review before it is relied on in legal work.Lylo is being designed to keep the solicitor in control, with checking, editing and review built into the workflow.
Client consent & transparencyFirms should consider when clients need to be informed or consent may be required for particular AI uses.Lylo is being designed as a controlled workflow so firms can decide where AI is used and how it fits their process.
Professional dutiesUsing AI does not remove the professional duties that apply to solicitors and firms.Lylo supports your informed decisions.
UK GDPR works alongside these professional responsibilities. The wider UK framework also includes the Data Protection Act 2018, the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, and PECR 2003 where relevant.Lylo is designed with these responsibilities in mind from the beginning.
