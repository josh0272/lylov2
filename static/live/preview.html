Firm-controlled by design

Private AI, designed to run inside your firm.

Lylo is being developed so legal work can use AI without confidential case material being sent to a public AI service.

Private deploymentClient information remains within the agreed firm-controlled environment.
No training on your case dataFirm documents are not used to train the underlying public model.
Source-linked outputsLylo can show the evidence behind an answer instead of asking the solicitor to trust a black box.

The precise safeguards depend on each firm’s deployment, access controls, policies and use. Lylo is being developed around privacy by design, data minimisation and controlled access.

Lylo is a working proof of concept. We’re now looking for a small number of firms to help shape how it develops into a production system.

Professional control

AI supports. You decide.

Lylo is designed to reduce the work around legal judgement, not replace it. Drafts, calculations and answers remain available for the solicitor to review, edit and approve.
