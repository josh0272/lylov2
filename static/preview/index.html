Built around real legal work.

Lylo is being developed to work with the information already inside a case — helping firms search files, prepare standard documents, calculate losses and handle routine client contact.

Work with the whole case, not one document at a time.

Upload witness statements, correspondence, pleadings and supporting documents. Ask Lylo a question in plain English and it searches across the file set for the information that matters.

Relevant facts can be brought back with the underlying source material available for review, so the solicitor can check where the answer came from.

Designed to reduce the time spent opening files, searching documents and rebuilding the same case context by hand.

Turn case documents into a draft form.

Lylo can identify names, dates, employment details and other required information from the documents already in the case, then place that information into the correct fields of a standard form.

The demo uses an ET1 as the example. The same workflow can be developed for other standard, repetitive forms used by a firm — with the completed draft kept for human review before it is submitted, exported or passed on.

ET1 is the example of choice, not the limit: the form workflow can be adapted around the fields, rules and documents used in other repeat legal processes.

Build a Schedule of Loss from the case information.

Lylo can use earnings, dates, periods of loss and other relevant figures to calculate the claimant’s losses and structure them into a clear Schedule of Loss.

The calculation can remain visible and reviewable, allowing the solicitor to check the inputs, assumptions and individual heads of loss before relying on the final figure.

The aim is not a hidden number. It is a repeatable calculation that can be checked, corrected and understood.

A realistic AI receptionist when your team cannot answer.

Lylo can speak naturally with callers, ask why they are calling, collect their contact details and gather the first information the firm needs before passing the message to the team.

It can be used after hours or when staff are busy. It also shows how the wider Lylo system can support general office work beyond the private case-file tools.
